About – Julian Schievelkamp

About the Artist

Julian Schievelkamp (b. 1994) makes images of things that don't quite exist. Based in Cologne, he moves freely between analog film and the computer, and his favourite part is where the two run into each other.

His work keeps returning to something that's hard to photograph: what it feels like when the senses blur. A sound has a colour, a memory has a texture, and everything you perceive folds into one single picture. Synaesthetic and psychedelic experiences have shaped the way he sees, and his images try to hold on to those moments just long enough to look at them.

Along the way the work has found an audience. It won the Prix de la Photographie Paris in 2017 and earned him the title Fine Art Photographer of the Year at the 5th Fine Art Photography Awards in 2019. It has been shown in London, Berlin and Cologne, and in more than 100 YellowKorner galleries around the world.

If one of his images stays with you: fine art prints are available in various formats and sizes. For collaborations, commissions or just to say hello, reach out via email or social media.
