Julian Schievelkamp (b. 1994) is a contemporary visual artist and fine art photographer based in Cologne, Germany. Using both analog film and digital techniques, his recent work emphasizes the synergies of classic photography and computer-generated imagery. Synaesthetic and psychedelic experiences, where sensory impressions merge into a single picture, have had a great influence on his way of working.

Schievelkamp has won several prizes and awards, including the 2017 Prix de la Photographie Paris. In 2019 the artist was awarded the title Fine Art Photographer of the Year by the jury of the 5th Fine Art Photography Awards. He has exhibited his work in London, Berlin and Cologne and over 100 YellowKorner galleries worldwide.

High-quality fine art prints in various formats and sizes are available upon request. For collaborations, commissions, or inquiries, feel free to reach out via email or social media.
